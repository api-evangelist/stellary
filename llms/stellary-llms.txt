# Stellary

> Stellary is AI-powered project management software for delivery, documentation, MCP, and AI agents. Centralize context, guide execution, and pilot projects from one command center.

Stellary unifies cards, documents, cockpit-style piloting, automations, and specialized AI agents inside the same workspace, so humans and agents share one operational source of truth — instead of working from disconnected tools.
